# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

App name: Nepal HelpLine  
Publisher: Bishow Adhikari  
iOS Bundle ID: com.bishow.nepalhelpline

Nepal HelpLine is an offline-first public phone directory and emergency contact app for Nepal. The app is designed to help users quickly find emergency numbers, public service contacts, hospitals, ambulance services, police contacts, government offices, utilities, tourism contacts, ward offices, and related public information.

Nepal HelpLine is an independent directory app. It does not represent, endorse, or act on behalf of any government entity.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how it is stored, and how users can control it. Nepal HelpLine is designed to work without login, without advertising, and without unnecessary personal information collection.

## Information the app provides

The app displays public contact directory information such as organisation names, public phone numbers, hotline numbers, public email addresses, websites, addresses, service areas, categories, verification status, source type, source URL, notes, and last checked information where available.

This public directory data is not personal information entered by the user. It is bundled with the app and may also be updated from a remote public data file.

The public source index used by the app is available at:

https://nepal-helpline-data.pages.dev/docs/official_sources.md

## Information you may choose to provide

You may optionally add personal emergency contacts. This may include a contact name, phone number, relationship or note, preferred action such as call or text, and an optional photo selected from your device.

Personal emergency contacts and selected contact photos are stored locally on your device in the current version. They are not uploaded to Nepal HelpLine servers.

You may optionally choose a default address mode, including manual location, GPS/current location, or last searched location. Manual and last searched location preferences are stored locally on your device.

You may submit correction suggestions inside the app. In the current version, correction suggestions are saved locally on your device as pending review and do not automatically update the public directory database.

## Permissions

Nepal HelpLine may request internet access to download updated public directory data, open public websites, and open online legal documents. If internet is unavailable, the app continues using cached local data or bundled offline seed data.

Nepal HelpLine may request location permission when you choose to use GPS/current location features. Location may be used to autofill province, district, or palika filters. The app does not request background location access and does not continuously track your location.

To convert GPS coordinates into an address or area name, the app may use device platform geocoding services. Depending on your device and operating system, this may involve services provided by Apple or other platform providers, which may process location information according to their own privacy policies.

Nepal HelpLine may request photo library access if you choose to add a photo to a personal emergency contact. The selected image is copied into app storage on your device and used only as the contact thumbnail in the app.

## Local storage

Nepal HelpLine uses local device storage to keep the app fast and useful offline. The app may store bundled and cached public directory contacts, latest successful remote directory data, data version and last sync time, search/location preferences, manual default location, last searched location, personal emergency contacts, personal emergency contact photos, locally saved correction suggestions, and theme preferences.

Local data remains on your device unless you delete it, clear app data where available, uninstall the app, or remove it through device settings.

## Remote public-data sync

Nepal HelpLine may connect to a remote static data source to check for updated public directory data. The current remote data source is hosted through Cloudflare Pages:

https://nepal-helpline-data.pages.dev/data/manifest.json

Remote sync is used to update public directory contact data. It is not used in the current version to upload personal emergency contacts, photos, correction suggestions, or location preferences.

When the app contacts the remote data host, normal technical information may be processed by the hosting provider, such as IP address, request date and time, requested file URL, and basic logs needed for security, reliability, and delivery.

## Data sharing

The current version of Nepal HelpLine does not sell personal data and does not share personal emergency contacts, selected photos, correction suggestions, or saved preferences with advertisers.

The app may open third-party apps or services when you choose to call, text, email, open a website, use platform location/geocoding, or open an external link. Those third-party apps and services are responsible for their own privacy practices.

## Tracking and advertising

The current version of Nepal HelpLine does not include advertising SDKs, ad personalization, or third-party analytics tracking for marketing purposes. The app does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Data deletion and controls

You can delete personal emergency contacts inside the app, remove or replace personal emergency contact photos, change default address settings, clear cached public directory data where available, revoke permissions in device settings, or uninstall the app to remove app-local data.

## Accuracy of public contact data

Contact details are collected from public sources and may change. For urgent emergencies, call national emergency numbers first and verify critical services where possible.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app changes, legal requirements, App Store requirements, or data handling changes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, data questions, or correction requests, contact the app publisher through the developer contact email listed on the App Store product page for Nepal HelpLine.
