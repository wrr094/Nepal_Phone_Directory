PRIVACY POLICY

Nepal HelpLine
Effective date: August 21, 2026
Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nepal HelpLine handles information when you use the Nepal HelpLine mobile application for iOS and Android. Nepal HelpLine is designed as an offline-first public phone directory and emergency contact app for Nepal. The app provides access to public emergency numbers, police contacts, ambulance contacts, hospitals, government offices, utilities, tourism contacts, disaster contacts, ward offices, and other public service contacts.

Nepal HelpLine is designed to work without user login, without ads, and without unnecessary personal information collection.

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMERS

Nepal HelpLine is an independent app. Nepal HelpLine is not a government entity, is not operated by the Government of Nepal, and does not represent any government department, police authority, emergency service, hospital, utility provider, ward office, local government office, or public authority.

Contact details shown in the app are collected from public sources and may change without notice. Although the app attempts to show source, verification, confidence, and last checked information where available, Nepal HelpLine cannot guarantee that every contact number, address, office name, working hour, website, email address, or source link is complete, current, accurate, or available at all times.

For urgent emergencies, call national emergency numbers first and follow instructions from official emergency responders. Do not rely only on this app for life-threatening, medical, legal, safety, disaster, or other critical situations. Users should verify critical services directly with official sources where possible.

This Privacy Policy is provided for transparency and general information. It is not legal advice. If you need legal advice about your rights or obligations, please consult a qualified legal professional.

1. INFORMATION THE APP DOES NOT REQUIRE

Nepal HelpLine does not require normal users to create an account.

The current version of the app does not require login, password, date of birth, citizenship number, passport number, national ID number, payment information, biometric information, health records, or other sensitive identity information.

The current version of the app does not include advertising SDKs, ad personalization, or third-party marketing trackers.

The current version of the app does not sell personal information.

The current version of the app does not upload your personal emergency contacts, selected personal contact photos, saved default address preferences, or locally saved correction suggestions to a Nepal HelpLine server.

2. PUBLIC DIRECTORY DATA

Nepal HelpLine displays public directory contact information. This may include:

- Organisation name
- Nepali name, if available
- Category and subcategory
- Description
- Province, district, palika, ward, and address
- Phone numbers and hotline codes
- Fax, email, and website, if available
- Department and working hours
- Latitude and longitude, if available
- Source name, source URL, and source type
- Verification status
- Confidence level
- Last checked date
- Public notes

This public directory data is intended to help users find public service and emergency contacts. The data is not treated as private user data. However, some public directory entries may contain names or official contact details of public offices, departments, institutions, or key persons where that information is publicly available.

3. PERSONAL EMERGENCY CONTACTS

The app allows you to optionally save personal emergency contacts, such as a spouse, family member, friend, or trusted contact. If you use this feature, you may enter:

- Contact name
- Phone number
- Relationship or notes
- Preferred action, such as call or text message
- Optional contact photo selected from your device

Personal emergency contacts are stored locally on your device in the current version of the app. They are not uploaded to Nepal HelpLine servers by the current version.

If you delete a personal emergency contact in the app, the app asks for confirmation before deleting it.

If you uninstall the app or clear the app's local data through device settings, your locally stored personal emergency contacts may be deleted from your device.

4. PERSONAL EMERGENCY CONTACT PHOTOS

If you choose to add a photo to a personal emergency contact, the app may ask permission to access photos or media on your device. The selected image is copied into the app's local storage and used only as the contact thumbnail inside Nepal HelpLine.

The current version of Nepal HelpLine does not upload personal emergency contact photos to Nepal HelpLine servers.

You can remove or replace a personal emergency contact photo inside the app where the feature is available. You can also revoke photo permissions from your device settings.

5. LOCATION AND DEFAULT ADDRESS FEATURES

The app may offer location-related features, including:

- Manual default address selection
- GPS/current location selection
- Last searched location
- Province, district, and palika autofill
- Location-based filtering of public directory contacts

Manual default address and last searched location preferences are stored locally on your device.

If you choose to use GPS/current location, the app may request location permission from your device. Location may be used to help match your approximate current area to province, district, palika, or other location filters in the directory.

Nepal HelpLine does not request background location access in the current version. The app does not continuously track your location. The app uses location only when you choose a feature that requires current location or when your selected default address setting uses GPS/current location for autofill.

To convert GPS coordinates into an address or area name, the app may use device platform geocoding or location services. Depending on your device and operating system, these services may be provided by Apple, Google, Android system services, or other platform providers. Those providers may process location information according to their own privacy policies and settings.

You can disable location permission at any time from your device settings. If location permission is disabled, GPS-based autofill may not work, but the app should still work with manual search, manual location selection, cached data, and bundled offline data.

6. CORRECTION SUGGESTIONS

The app may allow you to create correction suggestions, such as:

- Wrong number
- New number
- Closed office
- Duplicate entry
- Missing organisation
- Other correction

In the current version, correction suggestions are prepared for review workflow and may be saved locally on your device. The app does not automatically update public directory records based on user corrections. Corrections should be treated as pending review until verified through appropriate sources.

If a future version allows online correction submission, this Privacy Policy should be updated before that feature is released, and the app should clearly explain what information will be submitted.

7. LOCAL STORAGE AND OFFLINE CACHE

Nepal HelpLine uses local device storage so the app can work quickly and offline. The app may store:

- Bundled seed public directory data
- Cached latest successful remote public directory data
- SQLite database records used for search and browsing
- Data version and last sync time
- Search filters and location filters
- Manual default address
- Last searched location
- Personal emergency contacts
- Personal emergency contact photos
- Locally saved correction suggestions
- Theme settings, such as light mode or dark mode
- Basic app preferences

This local storage helps the app show contacts immediately, perform offline search, and avoid blank screens when internet access is unavailable.

You can clear cached data where the app provides a clear cache option. You can also delete app-local data by using your device settings or uninstalling the app.

8. REMOTE DATA SYNC

Nepal HelpLine may connect to a remote static data source to check for updated public directory contacts. The current remote data source may be hosted through Cloudflare Pages or another static hosting provider.

Remote sync is used to download public directory contact data and related metadata, such as a data manifest or data version. Remote sync is not used in the current version to upload your personal emergency contacts, personal contact photos, saved default address preferences, or locally saved correction suggestions.

When your device requests remote data, normal technical information may be processed by the hosting provider or network services, such as IP address, request time, device user agent, requested URL, and basic server logs. Nepal HelpLine does not use this information in the current version to personally identify users, create advertising profiles, or track users across apps or websites.

If remote data cannot be reached, the app is designed to continue using cached local data. If no cache exists, the app falls back to bundled offline seed data.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND DEVICE FEATURES

Nepal HelpLine may interact with third-party or device services when you choose certain actions, including:

- Phone dialer when you tap a call button
- SMS or messaging app when you tap a text/message option
- Email app when you tap an email button
- Web browser when you open a website or source URL
- Maps or location services if location-related features are used
- Platform geocoding services for location matching
- App stores or external links for legal documents, source documents, or support information
- Cloudflare Pages or another static host for public directory updates

These third-party apps, platforms, websites, and services are responsible for their own privacy practices, terms, security, and data handling. Nepal HelpLine does not control how those third parties process information once you leave the app or use an external service.

10. CALLS, TEXT MESSAGES, EMAILS, AND EXTERNAL LINKS

When you tap a call button, the app may open your device dialer with the selected number. Your carrier or phone service provider may process the call according to its own policies and may charge standard calling fees.

When you tap a text/message option, the app may open your messaging app with the selected number. Your carrier or messaging provider may process the message according to its own policies and may charge standard messaging fees.

When you tap an email button, the app may open your email app. Your email provider may process the email according to its own policies.

When you tap a website or source URL, the app may open your browser. The website you visit may collect information according to its own privacy policy.

11. DATA SHARING

The current version of Nepal HelpLine does not sell your personal data.

The current version does not share personal emergency contacts, selected personal contact photos, saved default address preferences, or locally saved correction suggestions with advertisers.

The app may share information with third parties only when you choose an action that requires another app or service, such as calling, texting, emailing, opening a website, using platform location services, or accessing remote public directory updates.

12. DATA SECURITY

Nepal HelpLine uses local device storage and standard platform features to keep app data available on your device. However, no app, device, network, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your device with appropriate security measures, such as screen lock, device encryption where available, operating system updates, and safe handling of your phone.

Because personal emergency contacts are stored on your device in the current version, someone with access to your unlocked device may be able to view them in the app.

13. DATA RETENTION

Public directory data may remain stored locally until it is replaced by a newer version, cleared through app settings where available, cleared through device settings, or removed by uninstalling the app.

Personal emergency contacts and selected photos remain stored locally until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

Manual default address, last searched location, theme preferences, and other local settings remain stored until changed, cleared, or deleted with app data.

Remote hosting providers may retain normal technical logs according to their own retention practices.

14. USER CHOICES AND CONTROLS

You can control many privacy-related features directly from the app or your device settings. Depending on your device and app version, you may be able to:

- Use the app without creating an account
- Search and browse contacts offline
- Add, edit, or delete personal emergency contacts
- Add, remove, or replace personal emergency contact photos
- Choose call or text as the preferred action for personal emergency contacts
- Select manual default address, GPS/current location, or last searched location
- Clear cache where the app provides that option
- Revoke location permission in device settings
- Revoke photo permission in device settings
- Delete app-local data in device settings
- Uninstall the app

If you revoke a permission, related features may stop working, but the main directory should continue to work with local and offline data where available.

15. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Nepal HelpLine is a public service directory app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app may include public child helpline numbers and women and children service contacts for safety and public information purposes.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration because normal users do not create accounts.

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of phones, emergency calling, external links, and messaging features.

16. GOVERNMENT INFORMATION AND NON-AFFILIATION

Nepal HelpLine may show government-related public information, such as government office contacts, ward office contacts, complaint numbers, ministry contacts, police contacts, disaster contacts, and other public service contacts.

Nepal HelpLine is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or operated by any government entity unless explicitly stated in writing by that government entity. The app provides source links where available so users can review original sources.

If any listing appears inaccurate, outdated, incomplete, misleading, or incorrectly sourced, users are encouraged to verify directly with official sources and submit a correction suggestion where available.

17. ACCURACY, AVAILABILITY, AND EMERGENCY USE

Nepal HelpLine makes reasonable efforts to support clear source, verification, and last checked information, but public contact information can change. Phone lines may be busy, unavailable, discontinued, misprinted by public sources, or unavailable in some areas.

Nepal HelpLine does not guarantee uninterrupted access to remote updates, third-party websites, phone networks, emergency services, internet services, or device features.

For immediate emergencies, use national emergency numbers first and follow official instructions. If one number does not work, try another official emergency channel, nearby public authority, hospital, police office, local government office, or trusted emergency responder.

18. INTERNATIONAL USERS

Nepal HelpLine is focused on public contacts in Nepal. If you use the app outside Nepal, your device, app store, operating system, network provider, remote hosting provider, or third-party services may process technical information in other countries according to their own policies.

19. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app changes, legal requirements, store policy requirements, technology changes, new features, or changes in data handling.

If the app later adds account login, online correction submission, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud backup, push notifications, user-generated public listings, or any other feature that changes data handling, this Privacy Policy should be reviewed and updated before release.

The updated Privacy Policy may be posted at the app's privacy policy URL, inside the app, on the app store listing, or through another appropriate notice method.

20. CONTACT

For privacy questions, data questions, correction requests, or concerns about public contact listings, contact the app publisher through the developer contact information listed on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store product page for Nepal HelpLine.

If a source URL or public listing appears wrong, outdated, or misleading, please include enough detail to identify the listing, such as organisation name, district, phone number, source URL, or screenshot where appropriate.

SUMMARY

Nepal HelpLine is designed to be offline-first, simple, and privacy-conscious. The app does not require login, does not include ads, does not sell personal information, and stores personal emergency contacts locally on your device in the current version. Public directory data may be downloaded from a remote static source for updates and cached locally so the app continues to work offline. Location and photo features are optional and used only for the features you choose.
