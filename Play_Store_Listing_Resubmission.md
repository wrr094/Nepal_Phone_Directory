# Play Store Resubmission Text

Use this text when resubmitting after the July 22, 2026 Misleading Claims rejection.

## Short Description

Independent offline Nepal emergency and public service contact directory.

## Full Description

Nepal HelpLine is an independent, offline-first public phone directory for Nepal. It is not a government app and does not represent, endorse, or act on behalf of any government entity.

The app provides quick access to emergency numbers and public contact information for police, ambulance, fire, hospitals, utilities, tourism contacts, disaster contacts, ward offices, municipalities, rural municipalities, and other public service contacts. Contact details are compiled from public sources and may change. For urgent emergencies, call national emergency numbers first and verify critical services where possible.

Official and public-service source links:

- Complete source index used by the app: https://nepal-helpline-data.pages.dev/docs/official_sources.md
- Nepal Police: https://www.nepalpolice.gov.np/
- Kathmandu Valley Traffic Police: https://traffic.nepalpolice.gov.np/
- Ministry of Home Affairs: https://www.moha.gov.np/
- National Emergency Operation Center: https://moha.gov.np/en/post/national-emergency-operation-center
- Health Emergency Operation Center ambulance information: https://heoc.mohp.gov.np/information-portal/ambulance-nepal
- National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority: https://ndrrma.gov.np/en
- National Child Rights Council Child Helpline: https://ncrc.gov.np/pages/child-helpline-service--toll-free-phone-number/
- National Women Commission Helpline: https://nwchelpline.gov.np/
- Hello Sarkar public grievance portal: https://gunaso.opmcm.gov.np/
- Nepal Tourism Board: https://ntb.gov.np/contact-us
- Nepal Electricity Authority: https://nea.org.np/

Inside the app, contact detail pages show source name, source type, verification status, last checked date, and the original source URL where available. A Settings page also includes Official Sources, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, About, and Disclaimer documents.

Key features:

- Emergency numbers available from the home screen
- Offline search for public contacts
- Province, district, palika, and category browsing
- Call, email, and website actions where available
- Original source links on contact detail pages where available
- Local-first data with remote public-data updates when internet is available
- Personal emergency contacts stored on your device
- No login required
- No ads

Disclaimer: Nepal HelpLine is an independent directory app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorised by any government entity. Contact information is provided for public reference only and should be verified with the original source or relevant official service.
