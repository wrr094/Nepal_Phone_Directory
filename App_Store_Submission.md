## Description

```text
Nepal HelpLine is an independent, offline-first public phone directory for Nepal. It helps users quickly find emergency numbers and public service contacts such as police, ambulance, fire, hospitals, utilities, tourism contacts, disaster contacts, ward offices, municipalities, rural municipalities, and other public services.

Nepal HelpLine is not a government app and does not represent, endorse, or act on behalf of any government entity. Contact details are compiled from public sources and may change. For urgent emergencies, call national emergency numbers first and verify critical services where possible.

Key features:

- Emergency numbers available from the home screen
- Offline search for public contacts
- Province, district, palika, and category browsing
- Call, email, and website actions where available
- Original source links on contact detail pages where available
- Local-first data with remote public-data updates when internet is available
- Personal emergency contacts stored on your device
- Optional current location support for autofilling location filters
- Optional contact photo for personal emergency contact cards
- No login required
- No ads

Official and public-service source links:

- Complete source index used by the app: https://nepal-helpline-data.pages.dev/docs/official_sources.md
- Nepal Police: https://www.nepalpolice.gov.np/
- Kathmandu Valley Traffic Police: https://traffic.nepalpolice.gov.np/
- Ministry of Home Affairs: https://www.moha.gov.np/
- National Emergency Operation Center: https://moha.gov.np/en/post/national-emergency-operation-center
- Health Emergency Operation Center ambulance information: https://heoc.mohp.gov.np/information-portal/ambulance-nepal
- National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority: https://ndrrma.gov.np/en
- National Child Rights Council Child Helpline: https://ncrc.gov.np/pages/child-helpline-service--toll-free-phone-number/
- National Women Commission Helpline: https://nwchelpline.gov.np/
- Hello Sarkar public grievance portal: https://gunaso.opmcm.gov.np/
- Nepal Tourism Board: https://ntb.gov.np/contact-us
- Nepal Electricity Authority: https://nea.org.np/

Inside the app, contact detail pages show source name, source type, verification status, last checked date, and the original source URL where available. Settings also includes Official Sources, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, About, and Disclaimer documents.

Disclaimer: Nepal HelpLine is an independent directory app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorised by any government entity. Contact information is provided for public reference only and should be verified with the original source or relevant official service.
```

## App Review Notes

```text
Nepal HelpLine is an independent public contact directory and emergency contact reference app for Nepal. It is not a government app and does not represent, endorse, or act on behalf of any government entity.

Government and public-service information is shown with source name, source type, verification status, last checked date, and original source URL where available. The full public source index is available here:

The app does not require login and does not include ads. Personal emergency contacts, optional contact photos, default address preferences, and correction suggestions are stored locally on the user's device in the current version. Remote sync downloads public directory JSON from Cloudflare Pages and caches the latest successful response locally for offline use.
```
